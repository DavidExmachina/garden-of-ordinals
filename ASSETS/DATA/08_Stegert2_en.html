Definition

  Stegert's Second \( \Psi \) refers to another ordinal collapsing function proposed by Jan-Carl Stegert in the same paper as Stegert's First \( \Psi \) in 2010[1].

  Since Stegert's First \( \Psi \) is also denoted as \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \), this OCF is denoted as \( \mathfrak{P}_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \) in the Calculate menu and the Explore menu for distinction. You can restore it by Options > Function Specification > \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \).

  The whole ordinal system consists of 4 functions: \( \varphi_\alpha(\beta) \), \( \alpha^+ \), \( \Theta(\alpha) \), and \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \) and the unnamed cardinal \( \Upsilon \) and they're described and defined as follows.

The Function \( \varphi_\alpha(\beta) \)

  \( \varphi_\alpha(\beta) \) denotes Veblen Function. It's defined as follows, as shown in the Help menu in the "Veblen Function" interface:

  01. \( \varphi_0(\beta)=\omega^\beta \)

  02. For a nonzero ordinal \( \alpha \), \( \varphi_{\alpha}(\beta) \) returns the \( (1+\beta) \)-th common fixed point of all the functions \( \gamma\mapsto\varphi_{\alpha'}(\gamma) \) for \( \alpha'<\alpha \).

The Function \( \alpha^+ \)

  \( \alpha^+ \) denotes the cardinal succession of \( \alpha \), which returns the least cardinal greater than \( \alpha \). For example: \( \omega^+=\Omega \), \( \Omega^+=\Omega_2 \), \( {\Omega_2}^+=\Omega_3 \) and so on.

The Function \( \Theta(\alpha) \)

  \( \Theta(\alpha) \) denotes the least \( \alpha \)-indescribable cardinal. For whatever reason, \( \alpha \) is restricted to limit cardinals in the ordinal system. This function is not present in Stegert's First \( \Psi \).

The Function \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \)

  \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \) denotes the collapsing function, which is the core of this ordinal system. Similar to Stegert's First \( \Psi \), this OCF takes an ordinal and an exotic mathematical object called "Reflection Instance" as input. Due to the complexity of Reflection Instances and Reflection Configurations, the definition of this function is extremely complicated, making it one of the hardest OCFs to understand.

  \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \) also returns the least element of the set \( \mathfrak{M}_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \), which contains ordinals \( \kappa \) satisfying conditions including but not limited to the following, depending on the Reflection Instance \( \mathbb{X} \):

  1. \( \kappa<i(\mathbb{X}) \), where the notation \( i(\mathbb{X}) \) will be defined later.

  2. \( \kappa\in \) some set (sometimes).

  3. \( C(\alpha,\kappa)\cap i(\mathbb{X})=\kappa \), where the set \( C(\alpha,\beta) \) will be defined later.

  4. \( \mathbb{X},\alpha\in C(\kappa) \), where \( C(\kappa)=\{\alpha|\alpha\in C(\alpha,\kappa)\} \) and the notation \( \mathbb{X}\in C(\alpha,\beta) \) will be defined later.


  The "Reflection Bracket Notation" and "Indescribable Bracket Notation" introduced in the help article of Stegert's First \( \Psi \) will also be used in this article.

The Set \( C(\alpha,\beta) \)

  The set \( C(\alpha,\beta) \), which is called "The \( \alpha \)-th Skolem closure of \( \beta \)" in the paper, is defined as follows:

  \( \begin{align} C_0(\alpha,\beta) := & \beta\cup\{0,\Upsilon\} \\ C_{n+1}(\alpha,\beta) := & \{\gamma|\gamma=\gamma_0+\gamma_1\land\gamma_0,\gamma_1\in C_n(\alpha,\beta)\} \\ \cup & \{\gamma|\gamma=\varphi_{\alpha'}(\beta')\land\alpha',\beta'\in C_n(\alpha,\beta)\} \\ \cup & \{\gamma|\gamma={\alpha'}^+\land\alpha'\in C_n(\alpha,\beta)\cap Card\cap\Upsilon\} \\ \cup & \{\gamma|\gamma=\Theta(\alpha')\land\alpha'\in C_n(\alpha,\beta)\cap Lim\cap\Upsilon\} \\ \cup & \{\gamma|\gamma=\Psi_\mathbb{X}^{\alpha'}\land\alpha'\in C_n(\alpha,\beta)\cap\alpha\} \\ C(\alpha,\beta) := & \bigcup_{n<\omega}{C_n(\alpha,\beta)}=C_0(\alpha,\beta)\cup C_1(\alpha,\beta)\cup C_2(\alpha,\beta)\cup\cdots\cup C_m(\alpha,\beta)\cup\cdots \end{align} \)

  Where \( Card \) denotes the class of cardinals, including \( \omega=\varphi_0(1)=\varphi_0(\varphi_0(0)) \), \( Lim \) denotes the class of limit ordinals, and \( \mathbb{X} \) in the definition should be well-defined.

  The only difference from Stegert's First \( \Psi \) is that the \( \Pi_0^2 \)-Indescribable Cardinal \( \Xi \) is replaced with \( \Upsilon \), and the function \( \Theta(\alpha) \) is also used.

\( \mathfrak{M} \)-\( \theta \)-\( \Pi_n^1 \)-Indescribable Cardinals

  \( \mathfrak{M} \)-\( \theta \)-\( \Pi_n^1 \)-Indescribable Cardinals are cardinals that are frequently used in this OCF, which is an extension of \( \mathfrak{M} \)-\( \Pi_n^1 \)-Indescribable Cardinals and is defined equivalent to the following:

  For a class of ordinals \( \mathfrak{M} \), a cardinal \( \kappa \) is \( \mathfrak{M} \)-\( \theta \)-\( \Pi_n^1 \)-indescribable (Or \( \theta \)-\( \Pi_n^1 \)-indescribable in \( \mathfrak{M} \)) if whenever \( U_1,\cdots,U_m\subseteq V_\kappa \) and \( F \) is a \( \Pi_n^1 \) sentence of the language of \( \langle V_\kappa,\in,U_1,\cdots,U_m\rangle \) such that:

  If \( \langle V_{\kappa+\theta},\in,U_1,\cdots,U_m\rangle\vDash F \), then there exists a nonzero ordinal \( \alpha\in\mathfrak{M} \) less than \( \kappa \) such that \( \langle V_{\alpha+\theta},\in,U_1\cap V_\alpha,\cdots,U_m\cap V_\alpha\rangle\vDash F \).


  If we omit \( \mathfrak{M} \), we can consider it as the class of all ordinals. If we omit \( \theta \), then we consider it as \( 0 \). If we omit \( \Pi_n^1 \), then we can consider it as \( \Pi_0^1 \).

  It's intended in the paper that \( 1 \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-Indescribable Cardinals are also \( \Pi_0^2 \)-Indescribable Cardinals and \( \omega \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-Indescribable Cardinals are also Totally Indescribable Cardinals (\( \Pi_n^m \)-Indescribable for all natural numbers \( m,n \)). But whether that's the case isn't obvious.

  The definition of the "Indescribable Bracket Notation" on this website is also extended to handle \( \mathfrak{M} \)-\( \theta \)-\( \Pi_n^1 \)-Indescribable Cardinals. For example:

  \( [P_{(1,0)}] \) denotes the least \( 1 \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is equal to \( \Xi \) in Stegert's First \( \Psi \).

  \( [P_{(1,1)}] \) denotes the least \( 1 \)-\( \Pi_1^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is presumably also the least \( \Pi_1^2 \)-indescribable cardinal.

  \( [P_{(1,2)}] \) denotes the least \( 1 \)-\( \Pi_2^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is presumably also the least \( \Pi_2^2 \)-indescribable cardinal.

  \( [P_{(2,0)}] \) denotes the least \( 2 \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is presumably also the least \( \Pi_0^3 \)-indescribable cardinal.

  \( [P_{(3,0)}] \) denotes the least \( 3 \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is presumably also the least \( \Pi_0^4 \)-indescribable cardinal.

  \( [P_{(\omega,0)}] \) denotes the least \( \omega \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is equal to \( \Theta(\omega) \).

  \( [P_{(\Omega,0)}] \) denotes the least \( \Omega \)-\( \Pi_0^1 \)-indescribable cardinal, which is equal to \( \Theta(\omega^+) \).

Notable Differences from Stegert's First \( \Psi \)

  Despite its similarity to Stegert's First \( \Psi \), Stegert's Second \( \Psi \) has a lot of important differences other than the ordinals they can represent. The most fundamental difference is that the definitions of Reflection Instances and Configurations are way different.

  As a consequence, the way to obtain certain ordinals are different. For example, to obtain the least Strongly Inaccessible Cardinal \( [P_{(0,0)}] \), in Stegert's First \( \Psi \):

  \( \Xi=[P_{(1,0)}] \)

  \( \mathfrak{p}_{[\Xi,1]}^\omega=[P_{(0,0)}] \)

  While in Stegert's Second \( \Psi \):

  \( \Theta(\omega)=[P_{(\omega,0)}] \)

  \( [P_{(0,1)}]=\mathfrak{P}_{[\Theta(\omega),(0,1)]}^\Upsilon \)

  \( [P_{(0,0)}]=\mathfrak{P}_{[\mathfrak{P}_{[\Theta(\omega),(0,1)]}^\Upsilon,]}^{\Upsilon+1} \)

  As you can see, we need to go one more step because according to the definition, \( \mathfrak{P}_{[\Theta(\omega),(0,0)]}^\Upsilon \) is not in normal form.


  Also, for every limit ordinal \( \alpha<\Upsilon \), the Reflection Configuration of \( \Theta(\alpha) \) is defined as \( \mathbb{G}(\mathfrak{m}):=(\Theta(\alpha);P_\mathfrak{m};\epsilon;\epsilon;\Upsilon) \), which means, if we use these Reflection Instances and Configurations, the superscript has to start from \( \Upsilon \).

  The least limit cardinal \( \Omega_\omega \), which is the limit of \( \omega^{+++\cdots} \), is equal to \( \mathfrak{P}_{[\mathfrak{P}_{[\mathfrak{P}_{[\Theta(\omega),(0,1)]}^\Upsilon,]}^{\Upsilon+1},]}^{\Upsilon+2} \) in this OCF. However, we can't plug it directly into the function \( \alpha\mapsto\Psi_{[\omega^+,]}^\alpha \) because it's not in normal form. We can only use \( \Upsilon+\mathfrak{P}_{[\mathfrak{P}_{[\mathfrak{P}_{[\Theta(\omega),(0,1)]}^\Upsilon,]}^{\Upsilon+1},]}^{\Upsilon+2} \), in which case it will be greater than the ordinal that we want to represent.

  As a result, we have to use \( \Psi_{[\omega^+,]}^{\Theta(\omega)} \) to represent the limit of \( \Psi_{[\omega^+,]}^{\omega^{+++\cdots}} \), while \( \Theta(\omega) \) is much greater than the limit of \( \omega^{+++\cdots} \). Not only that, it turns out that for ordinals in the form \( \Psi_{[\kappa^+,]}^\alpha \) is much more complicated if \( \alpha<\Upsilon \).

The Cardinal \( \Upsilon \)

  The unnamed cardinal \( \Upsilon \) is defined as the least ordinal satisfying the following conditions:

  1. \( \forall\theta<\Upsilon\exists\kappa<\Upsilon \): \( \kappa \) is \( \theta \)-indescribable

  2. \( \forall\theta<\Upsilon\forall\kappa<\Upsilon \): If \( \kappa \) is \( \theta \)-indescribable then \( \theta<\kappa \)


  There is currently no known name that is widely used for this cardinal. According to the behavior of this cardinal in this OCF, it highly suggests that this cardinal is equal to or at least, acts like the least cardinal satisfying \( \forall\alpha\in Ord(\alpha<\Upsilon\Rightarrow\Theta(\alpha)<\Upsilon) \), which is the limit of \( \omega \), \( \Theta(\omega) \), \( \Theta(\Theta(\omega)) \), \( \Theta(\Theta(\Theta(\omega))) \) and so on.

  Since the Reflection Configuration of \( \Upsilon \) is undefined, it also highly suggests that this cardinal is a singular cardinal. For this reason, we have to obtain other ordinals by collapsing cardinals in the form \( \Theta(\alpha) \), instead of a single cardinal, multiple times.


  Stegert's Second \( \Psi \) isn't perfect. The paper contained a lot of errors that caused this OCF not to work. For this reason, the version of Stegert's Second \( \Psi \) here will have differences from the original paper. All changes from the original paper will be explained here. And in this case, the original version derived from the paper will also be provided.

  The ordinal \( \Psi_{(\omega^+;P_0;\epsilon;\epsilon;0)}^{\varepsilon_{\Upsilon+1}} \) is called the Large Stegert Ordinal (LSO). It's the upper bound of the PTO of \( Stability \).

The Exclusive Command "ref"

  You can use the command "ref", which is a command exclusively in this ordinal system, in the Calculation Interface. The command "ref ord" displays the complete form of the Reflection Configuration and the \( \vec{R} \) value defined using the ordinal "ord" in computer code. If "ord" is in the form (SC,...) (With C being an ordinal in computer code), which corresponds to the ordinal \( \Psi_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \), it will also display the complete form of the Reflection Instance \( \mathbb{X} \) and the definition of the set \( \mathfrak{M}_\mathbb{X}^\alpha \).


  Example1:

  Input: ref (T(v,(v,)))

  Console:

  \( [\Theta(\omega),{\alpha}_{0}]=(\Theta(\omega);{P}_{{\alpha}_{0}};\epsilon;\epsilon;\Upsilon) \)


  \( \vec{R}_{\Theta(\omega)}=({M}_{[0,\cdots]}^{<0}-{P}_{(\omega,0)}) \)


  Example2:

  Input: ref (S(T(v,(v,))),(v,(v,)),Y)

  Console:

  \( [{\mathfrak{P}}_{[\Theta(\omega),(1,0)]}^{\Upsilon},{\alpha}_{0}]=({\mathfrak{P}}_{[\Theta(\omega),(1,0)]}^{\Upsilon};{P}_{{\alpha}_{0}};\epsilon;[\Theta(\omega),(1,0)];\Upsilon+1) \)


  \( [\Theta(\omega),(1,0)]=(\Theta(\omega);{P}_{(1,0)};\epsilon;\epsilon;\Upsilon) \)


  \( \vec{R}_{{\mathfrak{P}}_{[\Theta(\omega),(1,0)]}^{\Upsilon}}=({M}_{[0,\cdots]}^{<0}-{P}_{(1,0)}) \)


  The set \( \mathfrak{M}_{[\Theta(\omega),(1,0)]}^{\Upsilon} \) contains ordinals \( \alpha \) that satisfies all the following conditions:

  1. \( \alpha<\Theta(\omega) \)

  2. \( C(\Upsilon,\alpha)\cap\Theta(\omega)=\alpha \)

  3. \( [\Theta(\omega),(1,0)],\Upsilon\in C(\alpha) \)

  4. \( \alpha \) is \( 1 \)-\( \Pi_{0}^1 \)-indescribable

  5. \( \alpha \) is \( 1 \)-\( \Pi_{0}^1 \)-indescribable

Notation

  And other symbols "\( + \)", "\( ( \)", "\( ) \)" and "\( , \)".

  We define the right part function \( R \) similarly as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T)\mapsto R(a,b)\in T \)

  01. If \( a<b \), return \( a \)

  02. If \( \neg a<b \), then:

  02.01. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), return \( R(a_1,b) \)

  02.02. If \( a\in T_s \), return \( \overline{0} \)


  We define the left multiply \( \omega \) operation \( \omega* \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T)\mapsto \omega*a\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), then:

  02.01. If \( \neg a_0<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1})) \), return \( Add(a_0,\omega*a_1) \)

  02.02. If \( a_0<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1}))\land a_0=\varphi_\overline{0}(a_2) \), return \( Add(\varphi_\overline{0}(S(a_2)),\omega*a_1) \)

  03. If \( a\in T_s \), then:

  03.01. If \( \neg a<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1})) \), return \( a \)

  03.02. If \( a<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1}))\land a=\varphi_\overline{0}(a_0) \), return \( \varphi_\overline{0}(S(a_0)) \)


  We define the divide \( \omega \) operation \( /\omega \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T)\mapsto a/\omega\in T \)

  01. If \( a<\overline{\omega} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02. If \( \neg a<\overline{\omega} \), then:

  02.01. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), then:

  02.01.01. If \( \neg a_0<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1})) \), return \( Add(a_0,a_1/\omega) \)

  02.01.02. If \( a_0<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1}))\land a_0=\varphi_\overline{0}(a_2) \), return \( Add(\varphi_\overline{0}(a_2[\overline{0}]),a_1/\omega) \)

  02.02. If \( a\in T_s \), then:

  02.02.01. If \( \neg a<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1})) \), return \( a \)

  02.02.02. If \( a<\varphi_\overline{0}(\varphi_\overline{0}(\overline{1}))\land a=\varphi_\overline{0}(a_0) \), return \( \varphi_\overline{0}(a_0[\overline{0}]) \)

  With the definition above, we have \( a=(a/\omega,R(a,\overline{\omega})) \).


  We define the least collapse function \( LC \) to generate a \( \Psi \)-term with all its variables being minimal:

  \( (a\in T)\mapsto LC(a)\in T \)

  01. If \( a\in Reg \), then:

  01.01. Let \( a_n=\begin{cases} S(a_{n-2}) & DY_n(a) \\ LB_n(a) & \neg DY_n(a) \end{cases} \)

  01.02. Return \( \Psi_{[a,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0} \)

  02. If \( \neg a\in Reg \), return \( a \)


  We define the replace function \( RP \) to replace a variable in a \( \Psi \)-term:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T,k\in\mathbb{N})\mapsto RP_k(a,b)\in T_m \)

  01. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. Let \( c_m=\begin{cases} S(c_{m-2}) & DY_m(a) \\ LB_m(a) & \neg DY_m(a) \end{cases} \)

  01.02. Let \( d_m=\begin{cases} b & k=m \\ Solve(c_m,a_m) & k\neq m \end{cases} \)

  01.03. Let \( e_m=\begin{cases} Add(S(e_{m-2}),d_m) & DY_m(a) \\ Add(c_m,d_m) & \neg DY_m(a) \end{cases} \)

  01.04. Return \( \Psi_{[a',e_1,e_2,\cdots,e_n]}^{e_0} \)

  02. Else, return \( a \)


  We define the root function \( Root \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T)\mapsto Root(a,b)\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. If \( a'=b \), return \( a \)

  01.02. If \( a'\neq b \), return \( Root(a',b) \)

  02. Else, return \( a \)


  We define the tail function \( t \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T,y\in T)\mapsto t(a,b)_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a<b \), return \( a \)

  02. If \( \neg a<b \), then:

  02.01. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), return \( t(a_1,b)_y \)

  02.02. If \( a\in T_s \), then:

  02.02.01. If \( a=\Upsilon \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02.02.02. If \( a=\varphi_{a_0}(a_1)\land a_0,a_1\in T_m \), then:

  02.02.02.01. If \( \neg Cof(a_1)<\overline{\omega} \), return \( t(a_1,b)_y \)

  02.02.02.02. If \( Cof(a_1)<\overline{\omega} \), return \( t(a_0,b)_y \)

  02.02.03. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m\land y\neq\overline{0}\land y<a \), return \( t(a_0,b)_y \)

  02.02.04. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  02.02.04.01. If \( a\in Reg \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02.02.04.02. Else if \( \neg LB_0(a')<a_0 \), then:

  02.02.04.02.01. If \( a'=\Psi_{[a'',a_1',a_2',\cdots,a_n']}^{a_0'} \), return \( \Psi t(a,b)_y \)

  02.02.04.02.01. Else, return \( \overline{0} \)

  02.02.04.03. Else if \( b={b_0}^+\land b_0\in T_m\land a<\Upsilon\land R(a,b)=\overline{0}\land b\neq y \), return \( t(a,b)_b \)

  02.02.04.04. Else if \( \neg FP_0(a)_y \), return \( t(a_0,b)_y \)

  02.02.04.05. Else, return \( t(L(a_0,a'),b)_y \)

  02.02.05. Else, return \( a \)

  Throughout this article, we set \( y=\overline{0} \) if we omit the variable \( y \). For example, we write \( t(a,b) \) for \( t(a,b)_\overline{0} \).


  We define the collapsing tail function \( \Psi t \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T,y\in T)\mapsto \Psi t(a,b)_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m \), return \( a_0 \)

  02. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  02.01. Loop with \( i=n,\cdots,2,1,0 \):

  02.01.01. Let \( c=Solve(LB_i(a'),a_i) \)

  02.01.02. If \( c\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  02.01.02.01. If \( \neg FP_i(a)_y \), return \( t(c,b)_y \)

  02.01.02.02. If \( FP_i(a)_y \), then:

  02.01.02.02.01. Let \( d=L(c,CC_i(a')) \)

  02.01.02.02.02. If \( d\neq\overline{0} \), return \( t(d,b)_y \)

  02.01.02.02.03. If \( d=\overline{0} \), then:

  02.01.02.02.03.01. Loop with \( j=i-1,i-2,\cdots,2,1,0 \):

  02.01.02.02.03.01.01. If \( Solve(LB_j(a'),a_j)\neq0 \), return \( t(Solve(LB_j(a'),a_j),b)_y \)

  02.02. Return \( \Psi t(a',b)_y \)

  03. Else, return \( a \)


  We define the fixed point function \( FP \) to determine whether a term is a fixed point:

  \( (a\in T,k\in\mathbb{N},y\in T)\mapsto FP_k(a)_y\in\{\mathbb{T},\mathbb{F}\} \)

  01. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. If \( k>R_v(a') \), return \( \mathbb{F} \)

  01.02. If \( k\le R_v(a') \), then:

  01.02.01. Let \( c=Solve(LB_k(a'),a_k) \), \( b=CC_k(a') \), \( t=t(c,b)_y \)

  01.02.02. Let \( r=Root(a,b)=\Psi_{[r',r_1,r_2,\cdots,r_n]}^{r_0}\land r',r_0,r_1,r_2,\cdots,r_n\in T_m \)

  01.02.03. If \( \neg t\in T_s \), return \( \mathbb{F} \)

  01.02.04. Else if \( r=\overline{0} \), return \( \mathbb{F} \)

  01.02.05. Else if \( t=\Psi_{[t',t_1,t_2,\cdots,t_n]}^{t_0}\land t',t_0,t_1,t_2,\cdots,t_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.02.05.01. If \( t<RP_k(a,c{[\overline{0}]}^*) \), return \( \mathbb{F} \)

  01.02.05.02. Loop with \( i=0,1,2,\cdots,n-1 \):

  01.02.05.02.01. If \( r_i<a_i \), return \( \mathbb{F} \)

  01.02.05.02.02. If \( a_i<r_i \), return \( \mathbb{T} \)

  01.02.05.03. Return \( Add(LB_k(a'),c)<r_k \)

  01.02.06. Else, return \( \mathbb{F} \)

  02. Else, return \( \mathbb{F} \)


  We define the last space function \( LS \) to determine the leftmost variable after some variable that has some "space" of at least its collapsing cardinal. If this returns \( 0 \), it means that such a variable doesn't exist:

  \( (a\in T,k\in\mathbb{N})\mapsto LS_k(a)\in\mathbb{N} \)

  01. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. Let \( r=0 \)

  01.02. Loop with \( i=n+1,n+2,\cdots,R_v(a) \):

  01.02.01. If \( \neg Solve(LB_i(a),UB_i(a))<CC_i(a) \), let \( r=i \)

  01.03. Return \( r \)

  02. Else, return \( 0 \)

Cofinality

  We define the cofinality function \( Cof \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,y\in T)\mapsto Cof(a)_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\overline{0} \), return \( a \)

  02. If \( a=\Upsilon \), return \( \omega \)

  03. If \( a\neq\overline{0}\land a\neq\Upsilon\land a\in Reg \), then:

  03.01. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m\land y\neq\overline{0}\land y<a \), then:

  03.01.01. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(a_0)_y \), return \( Cof(a_0)_y \)

  03.01.02. If \( \neg\overline{1}<Cof(a_0)_y \), return \( \overline{\omega} \)

  03.02. Else, return \( a \)

  04. If \( a\neq\overline{0}\land a\neq\Upsilon\land\neg a\in Reg \), then:

  04.01. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), return \( Cof(a_1)_y \)

  04.02. If \( a=\varphi_{a_0}(a_1)\land a_0,a_1\in T_m \), then:

  04.02.01. If \( \neg Cof(a_1)_y<\overline{\omega} \), return \( Cof(a_1)_y \)

  04.02.02. If \( \neg Cof(a_0)_y<\overline{\omega}\land Cof(a_1)_y<\overline{\omega} \), return \( Cof(a_0)_y \)

  04.02.03. If \( Cof(a_0)_y<\overline{\omega}\land Cof(a_1)_y<\overline{\omega}\land(a_0\neq\overline{0}\vee a_1\neq\overline{0}) \), return \( \overline{\omega} \)

  04.02.04. If \( a_0=\overline{0}\land a_1=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{1} \)

  04.03. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), return \( CP(a,IT(a)_y)_y \)


  We define the collapsing cofinality function \( CP \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T,y\in T)\mapsto CP(a,b)_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(a_0)_y \), return \( Cof(a_0)_y \)

  01.02. If \( \neg\overline{1}<Cof(a_0)_y \), return \( \overline{\omega} \)

  02. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  02.01. Let \( u=R_v(a') \), \( l_m=LB_m(a') \), \( b_m=CC_m(a') \), \( c_m=Solve(l_m,a_m) \)

  02.02. Let \( c_{Lm}=L(c_m,b_m) \), \( c_{Rm}=R(c_m,b_m) \), \( d_m=Solve(l_m,UB_m(a')) \)

  02.03. If \( a'={a''}^+\land a''\in T_m\land a_0<\Upsilon\land Cof(c_{L0})=\Theta(c')\land c'\in T_m \), then:

  02.03.01. If \( a'<Cof(c_{L0})\land(\neg\overline{1}<Cof(c_{R0})\vee b\neq\overline{0}) \), then:

  02.03.01.01. Let \( y'=a' \)

  02.03.01.02. If \( y\neq\overline{0} \), let \( y'=y \)

  02.03.01.03. If \( Cof(c_{L0})_{y'}<a' \), return \( Cof(c_{L0})_{y'} \)

  02.03.01.04. If \( \neg Cof(c_{L0})_{y'}<a' \), return \( \overline{\omega} \)

  02.04. Loop with \( i=u,\cdots,2,1,0 \):

  02.04.01. Let \( e_m=\begin{cases} c_m & m\le i \\ d_m & m>i \end{cases} \)

  02.04.02. If \( b=\overline{0}\land\forall_{i+1}^u x(c_{Rx}=\overline{0}) \), then:

  02.04.02.01. Loop with \( j=i,i+1,i+2,\cdots,u \):

  02.04.02.01.01. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(R(e_j,b_j))\land\forall_i^{j-1}x(Cof(e_x)=\overline{1}) \), return \( Cof(e_j)_y \)

  02.04.03. Else if \( LS_i(a')=0\land c_{Li}\neq\overline{0} \), return \( CPM_i(c_i,a)_y \)

  02.04.04. Else if \( LS_i(a')\neq0\land c_i\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  02.04.04.01. Loop with \( j=i,i+1,i+2,\cdots,u \):

  02.04.04.01.01. If \( LS_j(a')=0\vee Cof(e_j)\neq\overline{1} \), return \( CPM_j(e_j,a)_y \)

  02.05. Return \( CP(a',b)_y \)

  03. Else, return \( \overline{\omega} \)


  We define the main collapsing cofinality function \( CPM \) as follows:

  \( (s\in T,a\in T,k\in\mathbb{N},y\in T)\mapsto CPM_k(s,a)_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. Let \( d=s \)

  01.02. If \( LS_k(a')=0 \), let \( d=L(s,CC_k(a')) \)

  01.03. If \( Cof(d)_y<CC_k(a') \), return \( Cof(d)_y \)

  01.04. If \( \neg Cof(d)<CC_k(a') \), return \( \overline{\omega} \)

  02. Else, return \( \overline{\omega} \)


  We also define that \( Cof(\Gamma_{\Upsilon+1})=\overline{\omega} \).

Fundamental Sequence

  We define the fundamental sequence \( [] \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,n\in T,y\in T)\mapsto a[n]_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02. If \( a=\Upsilon \), then:

  02.01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( \omega \)

  02.02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), return \( a[n[\overline{0}]] \)

  03. If \( a\neq\overline{0}\land a\neq\Upsilon\land a\in Reg \), then:

  03.01. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m\land y\neq\overline{0}\land y<a \), then:

  03.01.01. Let \( b=Solve(\overline{1},a_0/\omega) \)

  03.01.02. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(b)_y \), return \( \Theta(\omega*Add(\overline{1},b[n]_y)) \)

  03.01.03. If \( \neg\overline{1}<Cof(b)_y \), then:

  03.01.02.01. If \( b=\overline{0} \), return \( LimCard(y)[n] \)

  03.01.02.02. If \( b\neq\overline{0} \), return \( LimCard(Max(\Theta(\omega*Add(\overline{1},b[\overline{0}])),y))[n] \)

  03.02. Else, return \( n \)

  04. If \( a\neq\overline{0}\land a\neq\Upsilon\land\neg a\in Reg \), then:

  04.01. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), return \( Add(a_0,a_1[n]_y) \)

  04.02. If \( a=\varphi_{a_0}(a_1)\land a_0,a_1\in T_m \), then:

  04.02.01. If \( a_1=\overline{0} \), then:

  04.02.01.01. If \( a_0=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  04.02.01.02. If \( Cof(a_0)=\overline{1} \), then:

  04.02.01.02.01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  04.02.01.02.02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), return \( Norm(\varphi_{a_0[\overline{0}]}(a[n[\overline{0}]])) \)

  04.02.01.03. If \( Cof(a_0)\ge\overline{\omega} \), return \( Norm(\varphi_{a_0[n]_y}(\overline{0})) \)

  04.02.02. If \( Cof(a_1)=\overline{1} \), then:

  04.02.02.01. If \( a_0=\overline{0} \), then:

  04.02.02.01.01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  04.02.02.01.02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), return \( Add(a[n[\overline{0}]], \varphi_\overline{0}(a_1[\overline{0}])) \)

  04.02.02.02. If \( Cof(a_0)=\overline{1} \), then:

  04.02.02.02.01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( Norm(\varphi_{a_0}(a_1[\overline{0}])) \)

  04.02.02.02.02. If \( n\neq\overline{0}\land n[\overline{0}]=\overline{0} \), return \( \varphi_{a_0[\overline{0}]}(Suc(a[n[\overline{0}]])) \)

  04.02.02.02.03. If \( n[\overline{0}]\neq\overline{0} \), return \( \varphi_{a_0[\overline{0}]}(a[n[\overline{0}]]) \)

  04.02.02.03. If \( Cof(a_0)\ge\overline{\omega} \), return \( \varphi_{a_0[n]_y}(Suc(Norm(\varphi_{a_0}(a_1[\overline{0}])))) \)

  04.02.03. If \( Cof(a_1)\ge\overline{\omega} \), return \( Norm(\varphi_{a_0}(a_1[n]_y)) \)

  04.03. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), return \( P(a,IT(a)_y)[n]_y \)


  We define the initial term \( IT \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,y\in T)\mapsto IT(a)_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02. If \( a=\Upsilon \), return \( \overline{\omega} \)

  03. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  04. If \( a=\varphi_{a_0}(a_1)\land a_0,a_1\in T_m \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  05. If \( a={a_0}^+\land a_0\in T_m \), then:

  05.01. If \( a_0=\overline{\omega} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  05.02. If \( a_0\neq\overline{\omega} \), return \( a_0 \)

  06. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m \), then:

  06.01. Let \( b=Solve(\overline{1},a_0/\omega) \)

  06.02. If \( b=\overline{0} \), return \( \omega \)

  06.03. If \( Cof(b)=\overline{1} \), return \( \Theta(\omega*Add(\overline{1},b[\overline{0}])) \)

  06.04. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(b) \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  07. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  07.01. Let \( u=R_v(a') \), \( l_m=LB_m(a') \), \( b_m=CC_m(a') \), \( c_m=Solve(l_m,a_m) \)

  07.02. Let \( c_{Lm}=L(c_m,b_m) \), \( c_{Rm}=R(c_m,b_m) \), \( d_m=Solve(l_m,UB_m(a')) \)

  07.03. If \( a'={a''}^+\land a''\in T_m\land a_0<\Upsilon\land Cof(c_{L0})=\Theta(c')\land c'\in T_m \), then:

  07.03.01. If \( a'<Cof(c_{L0})\land c_{R0}=\overline{0} \), then:

  07.03.01.01. Let \( y'=a' \)

  07.03.01.02. If \( y\neq\overline{0} \), let \( y'=y \)

  07.03.01.03. If \( FP_0(a)_{y'} \), return \( t(c_0,a')_{y'} \)

  07.03.01.04. If \( \neg FP_0(a)_{y'} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  07.04. Loop with \( i=u,\cdots,2,1,0 \):

  07.04.01. If \( Cof(c_{Ri})=\overline{1} \), then:

  07.04.01.01. Let \( r=RP_i(a,c_i[\overline{0}]) \)

  07.04.01.02. Loop with \( j=i,i+1,i+2,\cdots,u \):

  07.04.01.02.01. If \( \neg Cof(d_j)_y<b_j \), return \( r \)

  07.04.01.02.02. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(d_j)_y \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  07.04.01.02.03. Let \( r=RP_j(c,d_j[\overline{0}]) \)

  07.04.01.03. Return \( r \)

  07.04.02. Else if \( c_{Ri}\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  07.04.02.01. If \( FP_i(a)_y \), return \( t(c_m,b_m)_y \)

  07.04.02.02. If \( \neg FP_i(a)_y \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  07.04.03. Else if \( c_{Li}\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  07.04.03.01. If \( Cof(c_{Li})_y<b_i\land FP_i(a)_y \), return \( t(c_m,b_m)_y \)

  07.04.03.02. If \( \neg FP_i(a)_y \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  07.05. Return \( IT(a')_y \)


  We define the collapsing fundamental sequence \( P[] \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T,n\in T,y\in T)\mapsto P(a,b)[n]_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\overline{0} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  02. If \( a=\Upsilon \), return \( LimCard(b)[n] \)

  03. If \( a=a_0+a_1\land a_0\in T_s\land a_1\in T_m\setminus\{\overline{0}\} \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  04. If \( a=\varphi_{a_0}(a_1)\land a_0,a_1\in T_m \), return \( \overline{0} \)

  05. If \( a={a_0}^+\land a_0\in T_m \), return \( \Gamma(b)[n] \)

  06. If \( a=\Theta(a_0)\land a_0\in T_m \), then:

  06.01. Let \( c=Solve(\overline{1},a_0/\omega) \)

  06.02. If \( \neg\overline{1}<Cof(c)\vee b\neq\overline{0} \), return \( LimCard(c)[n] \)

  06.03. Else, return \( \Theta(\omega*Add(\overline{1},c[n]_y)) \)

  07. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  07.01. Let \( u=R_v(a') \), \( l_m=LB_m(a') \), \( b_m=CC_m(a') \), \( c_m=Solve(l_m,a_m) \)

  07.02. Let \( c_{Lm}=L(c_m,b_m) \), \( c_{Rm}=R(c_m,b_m) \), \( d_m=Solve(l_m,UB_m(a')) \)

  07.03. If \( a'={a''}^+\land a''\in T_m\land a_0<\Upsilon\land Cof(c_{L0})=\Theta(c')\land c'\in T_m \), then:

  07.03.01. If \( a'<Cof(c_{L0})\land(\neg\overline{1}<Cof(c_{R0})\vee b\neq\overline{0}) \), then:

  07.03.01.01. Let \( y'=a' \)

  07.03.01.02. If \( y\neq\overline{0} \), let \( y'=y \)

  07.03.01.03. Return \( PM_0(c_0,a,b,a)[n]_{y'} \)

  07.04. Loop with \( i=u,\cdots,2,1,0 \):

  07.04.01. Let \( e_m=\begin{cases} c_m & m\le i \\ d_m & m>i \end{cases} \)

  07.04.02. If \( b=\overline{0}\land\forall_{i+1}^u x(c_{Rx}=\overline{0}) \), then:

  07.04.02.01. Loop with \( j=i,i+1,i+2,\cdots,u \):

  07.04.02.01.01. If \( \overline{1}<Cof(R(e_j,b_j))\land\forall_i^{j-1}x(Cof(e_x)=\overline{1}) \), then:

  07.04.02.01.01.01. Let \( r=RP_j(a,e_j[n]_y) \)

  07.04.02.01.01.02. Loop with \( k=i,i+1,i+2,\cdots,j-1 \):

  07.04.02.01.01.02.01. Let \( r=RP_k(r,e_k[\overline{0}]) \):

  07.04.02.01.01.03. Return \( r \)

  07.04.03. Else if \( LS_i(a')=0\land c_{Li}\neq\overline{0} \), return \( PM_i(c_i,a,b,a)[n]_y \)

  07.04.04. Else if \( LS_i(a')\neq0\land c_i\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  07.04.04.01. Let \( r=a \)

  07.04.04.02. Loop with \( j=i+1,i+2,i+3,\cdots,u \):

  07.04.04.02.01. Let \( r=RP_j(r,\overline{0}) \)

  07.04.04.03. Loop with \( j=i,i+1,i+2,\cdots,u \):

  07.04.04.03.01. If \( LS_j(a')=0\vee Cof(e_j)\neq\overline{1} \), return \( PM_j(e_j,a,b,r)[n]_y \)

  07.04.04.03.02. Let \( r=RP_j(r,e_j[\overline{0}]) \)

  07.05. Return \( P(a',b)[n]_y \)


  We define the strongly critical sequence \( \Gamma[] \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,n\in T)\mapsto \Gamma(a)[n]\in T \)

  01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( a \)

  02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  02.01. If \( \varphi_{\Gamma(a)[n[\overline{0}]]}(\overline{0})\in T \), return \( \varphi_{\Gamma(a)[n[\overline{0}]]}(\overline{0}) \)

  02.02. If \( \neg\varphi_{\Gamma(a)[n[\overline{0}]]}(\overline{0})\in T \), return \( \varphi_{\Gamma(a)[n[\overline{0}]]}(\overline{1}) \)


  We define the limit cardinal sequence \( LimCard[] \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,n\in T)\mapsto LimCard(a)[n]\in T \)

  01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( a \)

  02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), return \( {LimCard(a)[n[\overline{0}]]}^+ \)


  We define the main collapsing fundamental sequence \( PM[] \) as follows:

  \( (s\in T,a\in T,b\in T,c\in T,n\in T,k\in\mathbb{N},y\in T)\mapsto PM_k(s,a,b,c)[n]_y\in T \)

  01. If \( a=\Psi_{[a',a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n]}^{a_0}\land a',a_0,a_1,a_2,\cdots,a_n\in T_m \), then:

  01.01. Let \( d=s \)

  01.02. If \( LS_k(a')=0 \), let \( d=L(s,CC_k(a')) \)

  01.03. If \( Cof(d)_y<CC_k(a') \), then:

  01.03.01. If \( LS_k(a')=0 \), return \( RP_k(c,Add(d[n]_y,b)) \)

  01.03.02. If \( LS_k(a')\neq0 \), return \( RP_k(RP_{LS_k(a')}(c,b),Add(d[n]_y,b)) \)

  01.04. If \( CC_k(a')<Cof(d)_y \), then:

  01.04.01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( b \)

  01.04.02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), return \( RP_k(c,Add(Re(d,CC_k(a'))[n[\overline{0}]]_y,b)) \)

  01.05. If \( \neg Cof(d)_y<CC_k(a')\land\neg CC_k(a')<Cof(d)_y \), then:

  01.05.01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( b \)

  01.05.02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), return \( RP_k(c,d[P(a,b)[n[\overline{0}]]_y]) \)

  02. Else, return \( \overline{0} \)


  We define the recursive sequence \( Re[] \) as follows:

  \( (a\in T,b\in T,n\in T,y\in T)\mapsto Re(a,b)[n]_y\in T \)

  01. If \( n=\overline{0} \), return \( a[b]_y \)

  02. If \( n\neq\overline{0} \), then:

  02.01. Let \( c=Cof(a)_y \), \( l=LB_0(c) \), \( p=Re(a,b)[n[\overline{0}]]_y \)

  02.02. If \( a<l \), return \( a[RP_0(LC(c),p)]_y \)

  02.03. If \( \neg a<l \), return \( a[RP_0(LC(c),Solve(l,p))]_y \)
